# Gameweek Prophet - Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the requirements for Gameweek Prophet, a mobile and web-based application designed to assist casual Fantasy Premier League (FPL) players in maximizing their weekly FPL points with minimal time investment. The application aims to solve the problem of casual players setting and forgetting their teams, thereby helping them stay competitive without requiring extensive time spent on research and analysis.

## 2. Vision

To be the indispensable Gameweek Prophet for casual Fantasy Premier League players, bringing a touch of magic to their daily routine by revealing effortless smart team decisions and foretelling competitive success – all within the time it takes to conjure their morning coffee.

## 3. Goals

* Empower casual FPL players to make informed team management decisions quickly and efficiently.
* Provide actionable insights and recommendations without requiring extensive FPL knowledge or time commitment.
* Increase user engagement and competitiveness in their FPL leagues.
* Offer a user-friendly experience across all targeted platforms.

## 4. Target Audience

* Casual Fantasy Premier League players.
* Individuals who want to improve their FPL performance with minimal time and effort.
* Users who prefer quick, data-driven insights over in-depth analysis from podcasts and other sources.
* Individuals with varying levels of technical proficiency, requiring an intuitive and easy-to-navigate application.

## 5. Platform Requirements

* The application must be available on all major platforms:
    * Mobile: iOS and Android
    * Web-based: Accessible through standard web browsers

## 6. Core Features

### 6.1. Player Recommendations

* Provide weekly player recommendations for transfers and captaincy.
* Recommendations should be based on a variety of data points sourced from the FPL API, including:
    * Past performance (points, goals, assists, etc.)
    * Upcoming fixtures and their difficulty
    * Player price and value
    * Forward-facing data such as expected points
    * Injury news and potential unavailability
* Present recommendations in an easy-to-understand format suitable for casual users.

### 6.2. Important Player Updates

* Highlight crucial player information that could impact team selection, such as:
    * Potential price drops
    * Injury status updates
    * Announcements of double gameweeks
    * Suspensions

### 6.3. Fixture Analysis

* Provide an overview of upcoming fixtures, highlighting potentially favorable or difficult matches for specific teams and players.
* Present this information in a clear and concise manner.

### 6.4. Team Selection Advice

* Offer guidance on potential team setups for the upcoming gameweek, considering factors like budget, fixture difficulty, and player form.
* This could include suggested formations or player combinations.

### 6.5. Interactive Dashboard

* Serve as the central hub for presenting key information and recommendations.
* Utilize a scoring system to allow users to quickly compare players.
* Offer a high-level overview of important updates and recommendations.

### 6.6. Scoring System for Player Comparison

* Implement a clear and intuitive scoring system to facilitate easy comparison of player potential for the upcoming gameweek.
* Consider an overall score or a system with key indicator icons/labels for quick comprehension.
* Allow users to drill down for a more detailed breakdown of the factors contributing to a player's score.

## 7. UI/UX

* **Simplicity and Ease of Use:** The UI must be intuitive and easy to navigate for casual users who may not be familiar with complex FPL analytics.
* **At-a-Glance Information:** Key recommendations and updates should be easily visible and understandable on the dashboard.
* **Clear Visual Hierarchy:** Use visual cues to highlight important information and guide the user.
* **Interactive Elements:** The dashboard should be interactive, allowing users to explore data and recommendations further if desired.
* **Consistent Design:** Maintain a consistent design language across all platforms.

## 8. Future Considerations (Out of Scope for Initial Release)

* Personalized recommendations based on user's existing team.
* Integration with user's FPL account for automated team updates (with user consent).
* Community features or league comparisons.
